## Bake It Forward

**Idea:** Someone who doesn't live in Denver can buy a seat at a future RiseUP bake. Sean then gives that seat to someone local who will attend in their place.

**Why:** Lets friends and supporters anywhere take part in RiseUP and support Urban Peak, even if they can't come to a bake themselves.

**Status:** Added to the site (Bake It Forward section, after the "I'd like to attend" button).

**How it works (decided 10.2.26):**
- Donor gives $100 on Urban Peak's donation page and adds "RiseUP Bake It Forward" to the comment.
- Donor emails Sean from the page, so Sean has their contact details.
- Seats count toward the 6-seat limit; if a bake is full, the seat goes to the next RiseUP bake.
- Who gets the seat is left open on the page.
- After the bake, Sean sends the donor a photo of the loaf baked in their honor (a photo of, or note from, the recipient is a bonus when they agree; no photos of Urban Peak youth).
- The Colorado HCTC tax credit is mentioned only as an optional note for Colorado taxpayers.
