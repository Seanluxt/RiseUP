# RiseUP Ideas

Notes on features to build later. Point Claude here at the start of a session.

## Bake it Forward

**Idea:** Someone who doesn't live in Denver can buy a seat at a future RiseUP bake. Sean then gives that seat to someone local who will attend in their place.

**Why:** Lets friends and supporters anywhere take part in RiseUP and support Urban Peak, even if they can't come to a bake themselves.

**Status:** Idea only, not started.

**Questions to decide before building:**
- How does the buyer pay? Through Urban Peak's donation page (like regular seats) with a "Bake it Forward" note, or some other way?
- Is the price the same $100 donation?
- Who receives the seat: someone Sean chooses, someone the buyer nominates, or either?
- Does the buyer get anything back, such as a thank-you note or a photo of the loaf baked in their name?
- Should the page show how many seats have been baked forward?
- Does the HCTC tax credit apply the same way for out-of-state buyers? (They may not pay Colorado state taxes, so the credit may not help them. Check with Urban Peak.)
